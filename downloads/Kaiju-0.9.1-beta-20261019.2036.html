Kaiju 0.9.1 beta

  • Erase sounds. Select a program, combi, wave sequence or drum kit and press Delete (or right click › Erase). Kaiju tells you first if anything uses it, and Cmd+Z brings it back.
  • Find unused sounds. The new "Unused" button marks the programs in a bank that no combi, set list or Drum Track uses, and the wave sequences and kits no program uses. You can erase them all at once.
  • WS & Drums tab. See the wave sequences, drum kits and Drum Track patterns a PCG brings, who uses them and whether their samples are there. You can also sort, compact and move them.
  • KARMA warning when imported sounds use User GEs that live in a .KGE file.
  • Automatic updates. From this version on, Kaiju updates itself (Kaiju › Check for Updates…).
  • Help › Send feedback… opens an email with your Kaiju and macOS versions filled in.
  • Open a sample bank (.KSC) on its own, and fixes in how wave sequence samples are counted.
