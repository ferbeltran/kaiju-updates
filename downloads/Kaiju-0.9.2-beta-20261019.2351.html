Kaiju 0.9.2 beta

  • Copy and paste between open PCGs works like Import. Pasted combis and programs bring the programs, wave sequences, drum kits, patterns and scales they use, and a short summary shows where everything went and anything worth knowing (KARMA .KGE, missing samples…).
  • Set list slots with their sounds. When you paste set list slots copied from another PCG, choose "Paste with their sounds": Kaiju brings the combis and programs they play (even from the library's other PCG) and points the slots at them.
  • Set lists in a separate PCG. If your PCG has no set lists (like a sound library whose set lists come apart), importing set lists now adds them to it.
  • Change a bank's type. Right click a user program bank › Change to EXi / HD-1. Its programs can move to other banks of their type first, and everything that uses them follows.
  • Copy, cut and paste wave sequences and drum kits in the WS & Drums tab.
  • "Copy samples" when saving now includes everything a paste brought, and pasting follows your setting for factory programs a library changed.
