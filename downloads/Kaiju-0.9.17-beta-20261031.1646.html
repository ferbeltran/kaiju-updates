Kaiju 0.9.17 beta

  • Welcome: a Welcome row at the top of the sidebar goes back to the welcome screen (recent files, Import, New blank PCG…) without closing your PCGs; clicking a PCG returns to it on the same tab.
  • Clearer wording:
    • moving a sound to a bank of the other type now says Incompatible Bank Type and how to fix it;
    • the panel for several selected slots: Copy (⌘C), Cut to Move (⌘X), Initialize (⌫), what each one does, and the selection shortcuts;
    • right-click menus say Cut to Move;
    • changing a bank's type (HD-1 / EXi) says that it initializes all the programs in the bank, and that ⌘Z undoes it.
  • While a PCG loads, the mascot shows centered (for a moment it appeared stuck at the left).
