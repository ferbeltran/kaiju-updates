Kaiju 1.0

The first release of Kaiju: thanks to everyone who tested the betas.

  • Files in the cloud (iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive) that aren't on your Mac yet are downloaded before Kaiju reads them. If one doesn't arrive, Kaiju tells you instead of waiting forever.
  • Cancel while a file opens. If it takes longer than usual, Kaiju says why it might be.
  • Hold ⇧ Shift when Kaiju starts to open it without reopening your files.
  • Sample Builder: loops and phrases on the same note (they all read as C3) now go one per key, in file-name order, instead of overlapping.
  • Sample Builder: Correct tuning can be turned off. When it's on, a detected note sets the Tune that puts the sample in tune (the audio isn't changed).
